300KB image questions

Why use 300KB instead of 100KB?

When the destination allows it, a larger target can preserve more detail and reduce the need for aggressive quality or dimension changes.

Is 300KB good for a website?

It can be a practical starting point, but the right target depends on display dimensions and the performance goals of the page.
