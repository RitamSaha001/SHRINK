Questions about reducing PDF size

What affects PDF compression?

Images, scanned pages, fonts, embedded resources, and document structure all influence how small a PDF can become.

Does Shrink send my PDF to a server?

No. Compression is performed locally in your browser.

Can I choose KB or MB?

Yes. Shrink's target-size controls let you work with a size expressed in KB or MB.
