What usually makes PDFs large?

Scanned pages

High-resolution page images can dominate a document's file size.

Photos & illustrations

Image-heavy PDFs often need more space than text-heavy documents.

Embedded resources

Fonts and other document resources can contribute to the total size.

Pick the smallest target that still makes sense

A 40-page scanned PDF might be impossible to make useful at an extremely small target, while a short text document may already be below the limit. Start from the requirement, but let the source document tell you whether that target is realistic.

Reducing PDF size questions

Can every PDF be reduced to the same size?

No. The contents and structure of the source document strongly affect what is practical.

Does Shrink upload my PDF?

The normal PDF compression workflow is performed locally in your browser.

Should I always choose the smallest target?

No. Use the smallest target that satisfies the destination while keeping the document fit for purpose.
