Why target size?

A quality slider answers “how much?” A target-size workflow answers “will this fit?” When the destination gives you a hard file-size ceiling, the second question is usually the useful one.

What makes a PDF large?

Knowing what is taking up space helps you set realistic expectations.

Scanned pages

High-resolution scans can contain a large amount of image data even when the page looks simple.

Photographs

Photo-heavy documents may remain relatively large because their embedded image data dominates the file.

Export structure

Fonts, vector objects, duplicate resources, and export settings can all affect the final PDF size.

PDF compression questions

Does Shrink upload my PDF?

The normal PDF compression step is performed locally in the browser on your device.

Can one PDF always reach the same target as another?

No. The source content matters. A mostly text PDF and a high-resolution scan can behave very differently at the same target.

Should I use the smallest possible target?

Not necessarily. Use the smallest target that your destination requires while keeping the document useful and readable.
