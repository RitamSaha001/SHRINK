Why Shrink focuses on exact targets

A generic “high / medium / low” preset can be hard to relate to a real upload rule.

When a website says “maximum 100KB,” the requirement is already expressed as a number. Shrink's target-first workflow is designed around that number, while still being honest that different source files can have different practical limits.
