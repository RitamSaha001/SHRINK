Studio note

250KB is often about avoiding unnecessary quality loss

When a site accepts 250KB, there may be little reason to force a photograph into 50KB. Preserve enough detail for the job and use the extra budget when it improves the result.

250KB image questions

Is 250KB enough for a web photo?

For many displayed images, yes. The right target still depends on dimensions, subject detail and how the destination uses the file.

Can I use PNG at 250KB?

Yes when the destination accepts it, although JPG is often more efficient for photographs.
