Good fit

When 10KB makes sense

Very small image fields, simple signatures, icons, and legacy upload systems can impose unusually low limits. For a detailed photo, a slightly larger target is often the better choice.

10KB image questions

Can every image reach 10KB?

No. A detailed photograph may need smaller dimensions or a larger limit to remain useful.

Is smaller always better?

No. Use the smallest size that satisfies the destination while preserving the information the user needs.
