Questions about exact image size

Does exact size mean every image will hit the number perfectly?

No. The target-size workflow aims for the requested limit, but the achievable result depends on the source image and format.

Can I use KB and MB targets?

Yes. Shrink supports target-size controls for KB and MB.

Is compression local?

Yes. Image compression runs locally in your browser.
