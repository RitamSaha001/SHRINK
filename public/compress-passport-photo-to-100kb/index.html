Important

A 100KB file can still be the wrong photo

Meeting a file-size limit does not automatically meet portrait specifications. The destination may require a particular crop, dimensions, background or format.

100KB passport-photo questions

Is 100KB better than 50KB?

Not always. Use the smallest target that meets the destination's rule while keeping the portrait useful.

Can I compress a phone photo?

Yes. Start from the original camera photo when possible, crop it appropriately, and then work toward the file-size target.
