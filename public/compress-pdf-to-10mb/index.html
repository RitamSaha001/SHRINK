Think of 10MB as a performance ceiling.

You may not need dramatic compression. A modest reduction can be enough to make an attachment faster to upload and easier to share.

Common reasons a PDF reaches this range

Full-page scans

High-resolution scans can make otherwise simple paperwork unexpectedly large.

Portfolio exports

Images embedded at print-oriented resolution can dominate a PDF's storage size.

Presentation exports

Slides with large photographs, transparent assets and charts can balloon quickly.

10MB PDF questions

Why not compress as small as possible?

Because a smaller file is not automatically better. The useful target is the smallest file that still serves the destination and remains readable.

Is 10MB enough for any PDF?

No. Large scans or portfolios can still exceed 10MB after reasonable compression.

Does the standard compression happen locally?

Yes. The normal workflow runs in your browser.
