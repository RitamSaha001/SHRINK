5MB PDF questions

Why might a 30MB PDF still be hard to reach 5MB?

Large embedded images and scans can dominate the document. The amount of visual information matters more than page count alone.

Is smaller always better?

No. A 4MB document that stays readable is better than a 900KB document with unusable images or tiny text.

Does compression leave the browser?

The standard compression workflow is performed locally in your browser.
