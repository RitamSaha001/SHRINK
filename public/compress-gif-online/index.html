Make a heavy GIF lighter.

GIFs can become large because they store many frames and a limited color palette. When a moving image is too heavy for sharing or embedding, the useful goal is to reduce the payload without making the motion distracting.

GIF compression questions

Why are GIFs sometimes huge?

Animated GIFs store many image frames. Longer animations, large dimensions and detailed scenes can multiply the file size quickly.

Can a GIF be made dramatically smaller?

Often, but the useful tradeoff depends on the animation. Fewer frames, smaller dimensions or fewer colors can all reduce size.
