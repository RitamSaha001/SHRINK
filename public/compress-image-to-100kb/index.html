Questions about image to 100KB

Can every image reach 100KB?

Not necessarily. The achievable size depends on the source image, dimensions, format, and visual complexity.

Does the image leave my device?

No. Compression is performed locally in your browser rather than by uploading the original image.

How do I compress an image to 100KB?

Select your image, enter 100KB as the target in Shrink, compress, and download the result.
