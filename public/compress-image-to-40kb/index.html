Practical note

40KB is a budget, not a promise

A flat graphic may fit comfortably while a busy camera photo may need a larger target or smaller dimensions. Shrink keeps the tradeoff visible so you can choose intelligently.

40KB image questions

Will every photo fit into 40KB?

No. Texture, noise, dimensions and format all affect the minimum practical size.

Should I resize before compressing?

When the destination displays a small image, resizing can often be more effective than sacrificing all of the compression quality.
