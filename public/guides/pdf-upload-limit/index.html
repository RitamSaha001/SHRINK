Upload troubleshooting

PDF upload rejected for being too large?

First find out what the destination actually requires. Then reduce the PDF toward that limit while keeping the document readable. The best result isn't simply the smallest file — it is the smallest file that still does the job.

Open PDF compressorTroubleshoot it

Check these four things

Maximum bytes

Look for wording such as “100KB maximum” or “2MB or less.” That is your primary target.

Page count

A long document may not realistically fit an extremely small limit without major quality tradeoffs.

Scans & photos

Image-heavy documents often need more space than text-heavy PDFs.

Format rule

Make sure the upload accepts PDF and hasn't separately restricted page count or other properties.

Fix it in the least painful order

1

Use the original

A clean source is better than repeatedly compressing an already reduced copy.

2

Target the real limit

If the portal says 200KB, work toward 200KB rather than trying random quality levels.

3

Protect what matters

Check small text, stamps, signatures, diagrams and scanned details after compression.

When compression isn't enough

Sometimes the correct fix is to start from a better export or smaller source scan. If the document contains too much image data, a tiny target may be incompatible with readability.
