Practical note

File size and passport-photo requirements are different things

Shrink handles the file-size side. The destination may still have independent requirements for dimensions, background and composition. Confirm the latest instructions before submitting.

50KB passport-photo questions

Will 50KB preserve a face clearly?

Often, at small display dimensions. The original image, crop, lighting and output dimensions make a major difference.

Should I compress first or crop first?

When possible, crop to the required framing before compression. Removing unnecessary pixels gives the compressor a more efficient source.
