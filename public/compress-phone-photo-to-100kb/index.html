Mobile tip

Crop before you crush

If a photo contains a lot of empty wall, floor or background, trimming that area can reduce the amount of information the final image needs to represent.

Phone-photo questions

Why are phone photos so large?

Modern cameras capture high pixel counts and rich image data. That is useful for editing, but often unnecessary for a small upload field.

Can I do this entirely on my phone?

Yes. Open Shrink in the phone browser, choose the image, set the target, and download the result.
