1MB PDF questions

Can every PDF reach 1MB?

No. A long or photo-heavy document may need a larger target to stay readable.

Is 1MB always the right target?

No. Use the smallest limit the destination actually requires.

Is compression done locally?

Yes. The normal compression workflow runs in your browser.
