Questions about PDF to 500KB

Will the PDF always be under 500KB?

Compression results depend on the document. Text-only PDFs can behave very differently from scanned or image-heavy PDFs.

Does compression happen online?

The compression work is performed locally in your browser rather than by uploading the PDF to a compression server.

How do I make a PDF 500KB?

Open Shrink, select your file, set 500KB as the target, and compress. The resulting file can then be downloaded to your device.
