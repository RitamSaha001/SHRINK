Why some PDFs are stubborn

PDF is a container format, so two documents with the same number of pages can have completely different sizes. A mostly text document can be lightweight, while a scan with photographs can be dominated by image data. The right target is therefore a practical goal, not a promise that every source can reach the same number.

500KB PDF questions

Can Shrink make every PDF exactly 500KB?

No. The final size depends on the source document and the compression techniques available for its contents.

Why choose 500KB instead of a percentage?

Because many real upload systems give you a hard size cap. Targeting the cap makes the workflow directly relevant to the requirement.

Is my PDF uploaded during compression?

The normal compression step is performed locally in the browser on your device.
