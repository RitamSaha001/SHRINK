Why two JPGs behave differently

A smooth studio photo, a noisy night shot, and a screenshot can have very different compression behavior even at the same pixel dimensions. Texture, noise, edges, colors, and image content all affect how much data the final JPG needs.

JPG to 100KB questions

Can every JPG become 100KB?

No. The source image may be too detailed for that target without changing dimensions or accepting more visible compression.

Does Shrink upload my JPG?
