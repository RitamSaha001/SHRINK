Compress JPG to 100KB

Shrink makes it easy to work toward a 100KB JPG limit. Choose the image, set the target size, and download the compressed file.

The compression step runs locally in your browser, so you can reduce the JPG without sending the image to a compression server.

Useful for

JPG files often need to fit strict limits for forms, applications, profiles, and website uploads. A target-size workflow is more direct than repeatedly changing a quality slider.

FAQ

Can I set 100KB as the target?

Yes. Shrink is built around target-size compression, though the achievable result depends on the source image.
