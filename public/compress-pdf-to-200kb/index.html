200KB PDF FAQ

Can I target exactly 200KB?

Shrink is built around target-size compression, but the achievable result depends on the PDF's content and structure.

Will my PDF be uploaded?

For compression, the PDF is processed locally in the browser.

What if the PDF is already smaller?

Keep the original when it already meets the limit, or use Shrink when you want to reduce it further.
