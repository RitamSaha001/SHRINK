Questions about PDF to 200KB

Can I always get below 200KB?

It depends on the source PDF. Image-heavy or complex documents may have a practical minimum size, so results can vary.

Are my PDF files uploaded?

No. Compression is performed locally in your browser. Your PDF itself does not need to leave your device.

How do I compress a PDF to 200KB?

Choose the PDF in Shrink, enter 200KB as the target, compress, and download the result.
