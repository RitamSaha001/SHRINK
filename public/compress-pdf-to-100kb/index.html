Questions about 100KB PDF compression

Can Shrink target exactly 100KB?

Shrink is designed around target-size compression, but the achievable result depends on the source PDF's images, fonts, pages, and structure.

Is my PDF uploaded?

For compression, the PDF is processed locally in your browser.

What if my PDF is already below 100KB?

You can still use Shrink, or keep the original when it already satisfies your upload limit.
