20KB image questions

Why is 20KB so difficult?

A typical photo contains far more visual information than a 20KB budget can retain. Dimensions, texture and format all matter.

Can every image reach 20KB?

No. Some images need lower dimensions or a larger file-size target to remain useful.

Does Shrink upload the image?

The standard image compression workflow runs locally in your browser.
