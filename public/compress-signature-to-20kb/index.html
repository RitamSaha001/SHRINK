Don't assume every form uses the same signature specification

20KB signature questions

Why is a signature easier to compress than a photo?

A signature often contains simple strokes and large areas of one background color, which can be represented compactly.

Can I use a PNG?

You can use the format the destination accepts. If the form wants JPG, save the final result in that format.
