Small-file studio · 30KB

A little more room at 30KB.

30KB sits between an extreme 20KB budget and more forgiving 50KB limits. It is useful when the upload field is strict but you still want a little extra visual headroom.

30KB image questions

Is 30KB enough for a photo?

Sometimes. A simple portrait at modest dimensions can work; a detailed full-resolution photo may not.

Should I resize first?

When a very small target is required, smaller dimensions can preserve more visible quality than simply crushing JPEG quality.
