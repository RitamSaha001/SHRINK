Questions about PNG to 200KB

Can every PNG reach 200KB?

It depends on the image. Resolution, colors, transparency, and visual complexity affect the minimum practical size.

Does PNG compression upload my file?

No. The compression is performed locally in your browser.

How do I compress a PNG to 200KB?

Choose the PNG, enter 200KB as the target in Shrink, compress, and download the result.
