Why PNG files can be large

PNG preserves image information very differently from JPG. Large dimensions, many colors, photographic detail, transparency and screenshot content can all increase the file size. When a strict 200KB target is required, the practical question is whether the image can fit that target without damaging the details the viewer actually needs.

PNG to 200KB questions

Can every PNG reach 200KB?

No. Resolution, content, colors, transparency and visual complexity all affect the achievable size.

Does Shrink upload my PNG?

The normal PNG compression workflow runs locally in your browser.

Should I convert PNG to JPG?

Not automatically. JPG can be effective for photographs, but PNG may be the better fit for transparent graphics and sharp screenshots.
